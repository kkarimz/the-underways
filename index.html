Field guide

Survive the Underways

Move & fight
Use WASD or the arrow keys to move. Holding a direction stops at an enemy. Press again to attack, then watch for the enemy's reply.
Healing
Tonics found in the maze are consumed on pickup, restoring up to 2 HP. A red bar appears above your explorer at 30% health or less.
Descend & escape
Find stairs down on floors 1–3 and the escape gate on floor 4. Each timed floor starts at 60 seconds. An hourglass adds 10 seconds. Blue runes point toward the stairs or gate.
Bring back loot
Escape with both relics for a score bonus. Enemies never drop gold. Escape to bank found gold and browse the shop after the run.
Practice
Choose Practice on the start screen to learn the maze at your own pace. Practice runs earn no ranked score or banked gold.
Debug
Explore without a timer or damage. Choose a level theme, and use the pause menu to switch themes or toggle the full-map reveal. Debug runs are unranked.
